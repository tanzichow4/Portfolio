# T04 plan: `Window` chrome (drag, minimize/genie, close, wobble, reduced motion)

Source: `docs/plans/00-requirements.md` (Current state, Assumptions 1-16, AC1-AC28), `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/design-decisions.md` (decisions 1, 5; sections B, C, D), `docs/plans/T03-plan.md`, `design_handoff_tanzi_os/README.md` ("Window chrome", "Interactions"), prototype `Tanzi OS.dc.html` lines 305-355 (drag, tilt, genie pose).

## 1. Goal and scope

**Goal.** Replace the T03 placeholder boxes with a shared, accessible `Window` chrome (titlebar with icon, title, labelled minimize and close buttons, and a content slot) that the user can focus, drag with pointer events, minimize with a genie-style animation and close. Wobble tilt while dragging, the fly-out on open and the genie are all disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and minimized windows are inert; window contents stay placeholders until T07.

**Already done, not redone:** `clampPosition`, `dragPosition`, `tiltFor` (`src/logic/drag.ts`); `raise`, `topMost`, `zIndexFor`; `WIN_META`, `WIN_IDS`; `desktopReducer` with `focus`, `minimize`, `restore`, `close`, `open`, `move`; `WIN_TITLES`; resize re-clamp.

**Out of scope** (carried over): window contents and real heights (T07); the Wobbly toggle, `wobble` in `State`, `tanzi-os:wobble` storage (T05); menubar and menus (T05); dock and aiming the genie at the real dock button (T06); any UI to restore or open a window (T05/T06); keyboard move/resize, focus return after minimize/close, Escape (T09); boot and Restart (T08); mobile below 760px (decision 1); re-clamp on resize (done in T03); edits to `design_handoff_tanzi_os/`.

## 2. Acceptance criteria

AC1-AC28 are copied from `00-requirements.md` with the same numbers and meaning (full text there). Short form:

- **AC1.** Each open window renders through shared `Window`: role `region`, accessible name = `WIN_TITLES[id]`, keeps `data-win` and `data-min`.
- **AC2.** Root: absolute at `x, y`, `WIN_META` width, `--radius-lg`, `--color-surface`, `overflow: hidden`, `transform-origin: top center`, z-index from `zIndexFor`.
- **AC3.** `topMost` window has `--shadow-lg`, others `--shadow-md`; focusing moves it.
- **AC4.** Titlebar: padding `8px 8px 8px 16px`, 12px `--color-neutral-400`, bg `--color-bg`, `cursor: grab`; accent icon (hello HandWaving, about User, exp Briefcase, proj Folder, term TerminalWindow, site Info, hidden from AT), title, right-aligned buttons.
- **AC5.** Dock windows: minimize (Minus) and close (X). `site`: close only.
- **AC6.** Native `<button type="button">`, `.btn .btn-icon`, 24x24, icon `--color-neutral-400`, name "Minimize <title>" / "Close <title>" built from strings in `src/data.ts`.
- **AC7.** Content slot below the titlebar with placeholder content; window keeps at least `WIN_META` nominal height.
- **AC8.** At 1280x720 and 1920x1080 at rest, positions/widths equal T03 (existing `tests/e2e/desktop.spec.ts` unchanged and green), at-rest transform is identity.
- **AC9.** `pointerdown` anywhere on a visible window raises it (highest non-`site` z-index, large shadow).
- **AC10.** Keyboard focus entering a window raises it.
- **AC11.** Primary press on the titlebar (not on a button) and move: window follows the delta, clamped by `dragPosition`. Buttons and body never start a drag.
- **AC12.** Pointer events only, pointer capture, titlebar `touch-action: none`.
- **AC13.** `pointerup`, `pointercancel`, `lostpointercapture` end the drag; dropped position is in `Desktop` state and survives close + `open`.
- **AC14.** At most one `move` per drag (on release); press/release without movement changes nothing; other windows unchanged during the drag.
- **AC15.** Dragging selects no text.
- **AC16.** Wobble on, reduced motion off, dragging: `rotate(<tiltFor(movementX, true)>deg) scale(1.015)`, sign follows direction, within +/-7. Release: identity with `transform .45s var(--ease-spring)`. Wobble prop false: no rotation.
- **AC17.** Minimize button or titlebar double-click (not on a button) dispatches `minimize`; stays mounted with `data-min="true"`. Double-click on `site` does nothing.
- **AC18.** Genie: `translate(vw/2 - (x + width/2), vh - 40 - y) scale(0.06)`, opacity 0, `transform .45s var(--ease-minimize), opacity .4s ease-in`. Restore: identity, opacity 1, `transform .45s var(--ease-spring), opacity .25s ease-out`.
- **AC19.** Minimized: `pointer-events: none` and `inert` immediately; not clickable, not tabbable, not in the a11y tree. Restore removes `inert`.
- **AC20.** Closed-to-open after `Desktop` first mount: renders in the minimized pose, then the normal pose on a later animation frame. First-load windows do not fly out. No `setTimeout`; works under `StrictMode`.
- **AC21.** Close dispatches `close`: unmounts, content state discarded, position kept.
- **AC22.** Reduced motion (read at interaction/render time): no rotation or scale on drag; minimize/restore opacity only; open with no fly-out. Inertness and positions unchanged.
- **AC23.** Buttons of visible windows reachable by Tab in DOM order, Enter/Space activate, `:focus-visible` outline visible and not clipped.
- **AC24.** Titlebar text >= 4.5:1 and icon color >= 3:1 against `--color-bg` in both themes, tested with the existing contrast helpers. No new color tokens.
- **AC25.** No mouse-only behaviour: minimize and close are buttons; double-click and drag are conveniences.
- **AC26.** Pure logic (genie transform, pose for drag/min/reduced state) in `src/logic/` with unit tests; `useDrag` in `src/hooks/useDrag.ts`.
- **AC27.** Under `StrictMode`, mount/unmount and a drag leave no stray `window`/`document` listeners, no pending animation frames, no console errors or warnings.
- **AC28.** lint, typecheck, test, build, format:check, test:e2e pass. No test deleted or weakened; the T03 "no buttons" test is replaced per Assumption 14 (section 7, task W5).

**Added AC (gap found):**

- **AC29 (added).** If a window unmounts during a drag (closed by another pointer, or `Desktop` unmounts), no `move` is dispatched afterwards and nothing is logged. A second end event for the same drag (for example `lostpointercapture` right after `pointerup`) is ignored, so AC14's single `move` holds.

## 3. Architecture

**Approach.** `Window` is a presentational component driven by props and callbacks; it holds only local drag state and the fly-out flag. The pose (transform, opacity, transition) is computed by a pure function from `{min, dragging, tilt, wobble, reduced, x, y, width, vw, vh}`. Drag follows the pointer with local React state inside `Window` (only that window re-renders) and commits one `move` to the reducer on release (design-decisions C). A new `WindowLayer` maps open windows to `Window` so tests can drive the real wiring with a harness reducer, since there is no open/restore UI yet. No reducer or `State` changes.

**Window markup.**

```
<section aria-label={title} data-win={id} data-min={String(win.min)} inert={win.min} aria-hidden={win.min || undefined}
  onPointerDownCapture={onFocus} onFocus={onFocus}
  style={{ position:'absolute', left, top, width, minHeight, zIndex, background:'var(--color-surface)', color:'var(--color-text)',
           borderRadius:'var(--radius-lg)', overflow:'hidden', transformOrigin:'top center', display:'flex', flexDirection:'column',
           boxShadow: top ? 'var(--shadow-lg)' : 'var(--shadow-md)', transform, opacity, transition, pointerEvents: win.min ? 'none' : undefined }}>
  <div className="win-titlebar" {...handlers} onDoubleClick={dockable && !onButton ? onMinimize : undefined}>
    <Icon className="win-icon" size={14} aria-hidden /> <span className="win-title">{title}</span>
    <div className="win-btns">[minimize if WIN_META[id].dock] [close]</div>
  </div>
  <div className="win-body">{children}</div>
</section>
```

- `left/top` = local drag position while dragging, else `win.x/win.y`. Existing inline styles stay inline so the T03 wiring tests (`style.position`, `style.left`, `style.zIndex`) keep passing. `hidden` is removed (Assumption 6); `aria-hidden` is added with `inert` because jsdom does not implement `inert`, so component tests can assert the a11y tree too.
- `onFocus` on the section is React's bubbling focus (focusin), AC10. The reducer returns the same state when already top or minimized, so repeated calls are cheap.

**`chromePose` rules (exact strings, for tests).** `SPRING = 'transform .45s var(--ease-spring), opacity .25s ease-out'`, `GENIE = 'transform .45s var(--ease-minimize), opacity .4s ease-in'`.

| Case                  | transform                                                                                           | opacity | transition                               |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| min, not reduced      | `translate(${dx}px, ${dy}px) scale(0.06)` from `genieOffset`                                        | 0       | `GENIE`                                  |
| min, reduced          | `none`                                                                                              | 0       | `opacity .4s ease-in`                    |
| dragging, not reduced | `rotate(${wobble ? tilt : 0}deg) scale(1.015)` (tilt clamped to +/-7, non-finite and `-0` become 0) | 1       | `none` (follows the pointer without lag) |
| dragging, reduced     | `none`                                                                                              | 1       | `none`                                   |
| rest, not reduced     | `none`                                                                                              | 1       | `SPRING`                                 |
| rest, reduced         | `none`                                                                                              | 1       | `opacity .25s ease-out`                  |

`min` wins over `dragging`. Identity is the string `none`.

## 4. Design

- **User flow.** Load: five windows with titlebars in place (no fly-out). Press anywhere on a window: it comes to the front and gets the large shadow. Drag the titlebar: the window follows and tilts with the pointer direction, then springs flat on release. Click minimize or double-click the titlebar: the window shrinks toward the bottom center and fades out (no way back until T05/T06). Click close: it disappears. Tab moves through the minimize and close buttons of visible windows; focusing one raises its window.
- **States.** Loading: none (synchronous render). Empty: all windows closed or minimized shows only the wallpaper (valid; recovery comes with T05/T06). Error: no async work; reducer and `chromePose` are total; `setPointerCapture` and `matchMedia` failures are caught and degrade to no capture / no reduced motion. Success: as in the flow. Transitional: entering (fly-out, two frames), dragging, minimizing.
- **1280x720 and larger.** Positions and widths are unchanged from T03 (AC8). Drag clamps keep at least 80px on screen and y in `[36, vh - 60]`. Genie target is `(vw/2, vh - 40)` at any size (Assumption 4). Mobile is not built.
- **Accessibility.** Region named by the title (not `dialog`, Assumption 1). Icon `aria-hidden`. Buttons are native, labelled "Minimize <title>" / "Close <title>", 24x24 (WCAG 2.5.8), focus outline kept inside the corner clip. Minimized windows get `inert` + `aria-hidden` + `pointer-events: none`. Placeholder body is not focusable. Colours reuse tokens already passing AA (neutral-400 on bg); AC24 adds an explicit chrome test.
- **Reduced motion.** `prefersReducedMotion()` read on each render and at drag start: no tilt, no lift scale, no genie translate/scale, no fly-out; opacity fades remain (shortened to near-instant by the existing global rule, Assumption 10).
